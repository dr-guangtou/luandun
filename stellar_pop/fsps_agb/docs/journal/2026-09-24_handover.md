## Problems / Blockers
- The bump signal is template-dependent (C3K 0.02 mag, LW02 0.11 mag SSP delta); the paper must state the template configuration.
- Per-family classifier and recovery gains were run at agb = 2 only; the agb = 0 control exists for the exponential family only.
- Model omissions: no C-rich TP-AGB in MIST, no nebular emission or dust, four metallicities, one Z per history, uniform time weighting; rapid-quenching base rate 0.84 to 6.38 percent depending on the quench form.

## Key Decisions
- Own numpy CSP integrator on cached SSP grids (FSPS SSP rebuilds cost 11 s per metallicity and `agb` change); log-spaced lookback sub-grid after a 45 percent disagreement with the bin-center scheme.
- agb = 2 spectra from linearity (2 S(1) - S(0)); LW02 rebuild after Q1 showed a tenfold template dependence.
- Surviving-mass sSFR (FSPS `stellar_mass`, remnants included); per-model RMS scatter metric; full-range purity grids; paired per-fold standard errors with three noise seeds.
