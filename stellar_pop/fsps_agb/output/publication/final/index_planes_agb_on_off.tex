\caption{%
The three spectral-index planes for a mock population of quenching galaxies without
(top row, ``AGB off'') and with (bottom row, ``AGB on'') thermally pulsing AGB light.
Each row shows D4000 against H$\delta_{\rm A}$ (left), D4000 against the H$^-$ bump
(middle) and H$\delta_{\rm A}$ against the H$^-$ bump (right); the bump axis is
inverted so that a stronger bump (a more negative index) is upward. The population is
2000 delayed-$\tau$ histories that quench exponentially, drawn from uniform
$t_q \in [1, 5.9]$~Gyr, log-uniform $\tau_q \in [0.1, 3]$~Gyr and a truncated normal in
$\log Z/Z_\odot$ (mean 0, $\sigma = 0.2$~dex, range $[-0.5, 0.2]$), each observed every
50~Myr from 1 to 13~Gyr after the onset of star formation (482\,000 epochs). Grey
filled contours enclose 68, 95 and 99.5 per cent of all epochs. The red filled contour
encloses 68 and 95 per cent of the rapid-quenching class, defined by
${\rm sSFR}(0$--$100~{\rm Myr}) / {\rm sSFR}(100~{\rm Myr}$--$1~{\rm Gyr}) < 0.1$ with
${\rm sSFR}(100~{\rm Myr}$--$1~{\rm Gyr}) > 10^{-10}~{\rm yr^{-1}}$ (1.2 per cent of the
epochs). Blue curves trace the fiducial history ($t_q = 3$~Gyr, $\tau_q = 0.3$~Gyr) at
$\log Z/Z_\odot = -0.5, -0.25, 0$ and $+0.25$ from $t_q$ to $t_q + 5$~Gyr, with markers
at $t_q$ and 0.5, 1, 2 and 5~Gyr later. Models are FSPS with MIST isochrones, the C3K
high-resolution library and a Chabrier IMF; D4000 and H$\delta_{\rm A}$ are measured
at $\sigma = 300$~km\,s$^{-1}$ and the H$^-$ bump at $R = 100$. ``AGB off'' removes the
TP-AGB stars (FSPS weight ${\tt agb} = 0$); ``AGB on'' assigns them the empirical
O-rich spectra of \citet{Lancon2002} (${\tt use\_lw\_tpagb} = 1$) at twice the default
weight (${\tt agb} = 2$). The optical plane is identical in the two rows, whereas the
AGB-on population lies 0.04 to 0.08~mag deeper in the bump and its rapid-quenching
class occupies the deepest part of the plane (median $-0.075$~mag against
$-0.004$~mag with AGB off). The metallicity trend of the bump reverses sign between
the two configurations.%
}
