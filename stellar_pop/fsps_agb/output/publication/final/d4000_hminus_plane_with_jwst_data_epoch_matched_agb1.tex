\caption{%
As Fig.~\ref{fig:d4000_hminus_plane_with_jwst_data_epoch_matched}, but with the
TP-AGB-on model at the default TP-AGB weight (${\tt agb} = 1$) instead of twice it; the
empirical O-rich templates of \citet{Lancon2002} are used in both. Model epochs are
restricted to the cosmic age at the redshifts of the \citet{Lu2026} sample
(3.3--5.7~Gyr); filled contours show these epochs and dashed open contours all epochs
from 1 to 13~Gyr, and the fiducial-history tracks are recomputed at ${\tt agb} = 1$.
Over the observed D4000 range the epoch-matched TP-AGB-on locus now spans
$-0.049$ to $-0.031$~mag (1st to 99th percentile), about half as deep as at
${\tt agb} = 2$. Only 6 of the 19 galaxies lie within its 99.5 per cent contour: the
weakest-bump objects, two of which (9234 and 22897) fall inside the rapid-quenching
contour. The other 13, including every galaxy with a bump stronger than
$-0.055$~mag, lie above the locus. The sample therefore spans the range between the
default and doubled TP-AGB weights, with most galaxies requiring the stronger
contribution; because the index responds to the weight and the template jointly, this
constrains their product for these templates, not the TP-AGB light fraction alone.%
