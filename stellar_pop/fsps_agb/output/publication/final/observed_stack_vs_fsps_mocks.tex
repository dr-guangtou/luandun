\caption{%
The stacked JWST spectrum of the quiescent sample compared with FSPS mock spectra
without (left, ``AGB off'') and with (right, ``AGB on'') TP-AGB light in the
H$^-$ bump region. Every spectrum, observed and model, is divided by a straight line
fitted to all pixels in the blue (1.494--1.539~$\mu$m) and red (1.746--1.791~$\mu$m)
pseudo-continuum windows (shaded blue and red; the feature band, 1.570--1.734~$\mu$m,
is shaded yellow). The black curve in panels (a) and (b) is the S/N-weighted mean of the
19 normalised rest-frame NIRSpec PRISM spectra of \citet{Lu2026} ($z = 1.0$--2.0) on a
common 30~\AA\ grid, and the grey band is the weighted scatter between galaxies. The
shaded model envelope spans every post-quenching epoch ($0 \le t - t_q \le 6$~Gyr,
$t \ge 1$~Gyr) of twelve quenching histories with a delayed-$\tau$ rise ($\tau = t_q$)
and an exponential decline, $t_q \in \{1.5, 3, 4.5\}$~Gyr and
$\tau_q \in \{0.1, 0.3, 1, 3\}$~Gyr, at solar metallicity and smoothed to $R = 100$;
the coloured curves follow the $t_q = 3$~Gyr, $\tau_q = 0.3$~Gyr history at 0.5, 1, 2
and 5~Gyr after quenching. Panels (c) and (d) show the observed stack divided by each
coloured model, with the grey band again the galaxy-to-galaxy scatter. Without TP-AGB
light no quenching history raises the normalised flux in the feature band above 1.03,
whereas the observed stack reaches 1.08 (an H$^-$ bump index of $-0.058$~mag against
model values between $+0.008$ and $-0.017$~mag), so the observed bump cannot be
reproduced by any star formation history in this configuration. With the empirical
TP-AGB templates the envelope (indices $-0.041$ to $-0.088$~mag) brackets the observed
stack and the ratio stays within the galaxy-to-galaxy scatter across the feature band.
The models are not fitted to the data: they are drawn at fixed metallicity and
resolution to show the range of quenching histories, and the model H$_2$O absorption
outside the side windows (below 1.45 and above 1.8~$\mu$m) is not tested by this
normalisation.%
}
