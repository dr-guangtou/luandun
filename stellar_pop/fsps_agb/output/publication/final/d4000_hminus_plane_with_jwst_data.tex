\caption{%
The D4000 versus H$^-$ bump plane of the mock population without (left, ``TP-AGB off'')
and with (right, ``TP-AGB on'') TP-AGB light, on a common bump axis, compared with the 19
quiescent galaxies at $z = 1.0$--$2.0$ observed with JWST by \citet{Lu2026} (black
points). Model layers are those of Fig.~\ref{fig:index_planes_agb_on_off}: grey
contours enclose 68, 95 and 99.5 per cent of all epochs later than 1~Gyr, the red
contour encloses 68 and 95 per cent of the rapid-quenching class, and the blue curves
trace the fiducial history ($t_q = 3$~Gyr, $\tau_q = 0.3$~Gyr) at
$\log Z/Z_\odot = -0.5$ to $+0.25$ from $t_q$ to $t_q + 5$~Gyr with markers at $t_q$ and
0.5, 1, 2 and 5~Gyr later. Horizontal error bars span the 16th to 84th percentiles of
D4000; the bump uncertainties (0.0002 to 0.0025~mag) are smaller than the symbols.
The observed galaxies span D4000 $= 1.35$--$1.94$ and H$^-$ bump
$= -0.037$ to $-0.073$~mag, inside the TP-AGB-on locus and several near its
rapid-quenching region, but 0.02 to 0.05~mag deeper than any epoch of the TP-AGB-off
model at the same D4000, whose deepest epoch over this D4000 range reaches only
$-0.023$~mag.%
}
